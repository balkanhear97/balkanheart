About BalkanHeart Scheduler

BalkanHeart Scheduler is a desktop-based content management tool designed for organizing and publishing original short-form videos.

How It Works

The application helps the account owner prepare videos, manage captions and hashtags, and schedule publishing at selected times.

TikTok account access requires authorization through TikTok's official authentication process. Publishing functionality is subject to TikTok API permissions and approval.
