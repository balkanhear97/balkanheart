Privacy Policy

BalkanHeart Scheduler

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

BalkanHeart Scheduler is a desktop-based tool intended to help its operator organize and publish short-form video content to authorized social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

When a TikTok account is connected with the account holder's authorization, the application may process information necessary for its publishing functions, including:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens.
  • Videos, captions, hashtags and publishing preferences.
  • Publishing status, timestamps and API responses.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare content, submit publishing requests and track publishing results.

BalkanHeart Scheduler does not sell personal information or use TikTok account data for unrelated advertising.

4. Storage and Security

The application is intended to operate locally on the operator's computer. Authentication credentials and publishing records may be stored locally to support authorized operations.

The operator is responsible for restricting access to the computer and protecting stored credentials. Data may also be transmitted to TikTok or authorized hosting services when required for publishing.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with TikTok's official APIs for account authorization and content publishing. TikTok processes information according to its own applicable privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored credentials, publishing records and associated content may be deleted when they are no longer required. Account holders may revoke the application's authorization through the relevant TikTok account settings.

7. User Rights

Account holders may request information about personal data processed by the application, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw authorization for future access.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data processing practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, please contact the application operator using the contact information published on the BalkanHeart Scheduler website.
